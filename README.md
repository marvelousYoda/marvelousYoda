# Hi, I'm marvelousYoda 👋

I'm a Product Manager at Microsoft who enjoys turning product ideas into working prototypes through hands-on, AI-assisted coding. I also contribute to Microsoft 365 technical documentation, helping make complex product experiences easier to understand and use.

## What I work on

- **Product management:** shaping useful product experiences and connecting customer needs to what teams build.
- **Microsoft 365 documentation:** making technical concepts and workflows clear, accurate, and actionable.
- **Prototyping:** exploring ideas by building small, working projects with code and AI tools.

## Projects

- [Copilot CLI Workshop](https://github.com/marvelousYoda/copilot-cli-workshop) — A hands-on lab that takes a product idea from specification and backlog through implementation, review, and a dashboard.
- [Mortgage Calculator](https://github.com/marvelousYoda/mortgage-calculator) — A React and TypeScript app for exploring monthly mortgage payments and interest over the life of a loan.
- [Microsoft 365 Docs](https://github.com/microsoftdocs/microsoft-365-docs) — The public source repository for Microsoft 365 documentation; I contribute to this documentation as part of my work.

## A little more

I like working across product, documentation, and engineering—using prototypes to make ideas concrete and clear writing to make products easier to use.
